## Vanderwolf's Pyramid Limber Pine

**Botanical name:** *Pinus flexilis* 'Vanderwolf's Pyramid'

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Evergreen conifer |
| **Mature size** | 20–25 ft tall × 10–15 ft wide |
| **Growth rate** | Slow |
| **Form / habit** | Narrow pyramid; dense, upright branching |
| **Light** | Full sun |
| **Water** | Once established, water deeply but infrequently |
| **Hardiness zone** | USDA 3–7 |

### Visual Details

- **Needle color:** Blue-green to silvery-blue, twisted needles in bundles of 5
- **Bark:** Smooth gray when young, furrowing into dark gray-brown plates with age
- **Texture:** Medium-fine; soft, flexible needles give a feathery look
- **Seasonal change:** Evergreen year-round; minimal color shift — slightly more silver-blue in winter
- **Silhouette:** Tapered pyramid, broader at base, pointed crown

### Landscape Context

- Works as a **specimen tree** or **privacy screen**
- Pairs well with low ornamental grasses or groundcover at the base
- Thrives in exposed, windy sites; tolerates rocky or poor soil

## Baby Blue Eyes Spruce

**Botanical name:** *Picea pungens* 'Baby Blue Eyes'

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Evergreen conifer |
| **Mature size** | 15–30 ft tall × 15 ft wide |
| **Growth rate** | Slow |
| **Form / habit** | Broad pyramid; dense, symmetrical branching |
| **Light** | Full sun to partial sun |
| **Water** | Water when top 2 inches of soil is dry |
| **Hardiness zone** | USDA 2–7 |

### Visual Details

- **Needle color:** Intense powder-blue to steel-blue; retains vivid blue year-round
- **Bark:** Gray-brown, thin, scaly
- **Texture:** Stiff, sharp needles create a coarse, rigid texture
- **Seasonal change:** Evergreen; new spring growth emerges lighter blue before deepening
- **Silhouette:** Classic Christmas-tree shape — wide conical base tapering to a pointed tip
- **Cones:** Light brown, cylindrical, 2–4 in long; hang from upper branches

### Landscape Context

- Strong **focal point** tree due to striking blue color
- Use as a **standalone specimen** or in a staggered row for windbreaks
- Contrasts well against dark green evergreens or warm-toned hardscapes

## Columnar Norway Spruce

**Botanical name:** *Picea abies* 'Cupressina'

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Type** | Evergreen conifer |
| **Mature size** | 20 ft tall × 5–6 ft wide (10 yr); up to 30 ft tall at maturity |
| **Growth rate** | Moderate |
| **Form / habit** | Narrow columnar; tightly upswept branches |
| **Light** | Full sun |
| **Water** | Water when top 2 inches of soil is dry |
| **Hardiness zone** | USDA 3–8 |

### Visual Details

- **Needle color:** Rich dark green; short, dense needles
- **Bark:** Orange-brown when young, maturing to gray-brown with flaky scales
- **Texture:** Fine to medium; tight branch density creates a solid, pillar-like mass
- **Seasonal change:** Evergreen; bright lime-green new growth at branch tips in spring
- **Silhouette:** Tall, narrow column — resembles an Italian cypress in form
- **Cones:** Elongated, pendulous, 4–6 in long; reddish-purple when young, brown at maturity

### Landscape Context

- Ideal for **narrow spaces**, driveways, or formal garden lines
- Use in **pairs or rows** to frame entryways or create vertical rhythm
- Strong architectural form; complements modern and formal landscape styles
