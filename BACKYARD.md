# Trees and Shrubs

## Vanderwolf's Pyramid Limber Pine

Pinus flexilis 'Vanderwolf's Pyramid'
LIGHT: Full sun
WATER: Once established water deeply occasionally.
SIZE: Slow growing; reaches 20 to 25 ft. tall, 10 to 15 ft. wide.

## Baby Blue Eyes Spruce

Picea pungens 'Baby Blue Eyes'
LIGHT: Full sun, Partial sun
WATER: Water when top 2 inches of soil is dry.
SIZE: Slow growing, 15 to 30 ft. tall, 15 ft. wide.

## Columnar Norway Spruce

Picea abies 'Cupressina'
LIGHT: Full sun
WATER: Water when top 2 inches of soil is dry.
SIZE: Reaches 20 ft. tall, 5 to 6 ft. wide in 10 years; grows to 30 ft. tall with age.
